Privacy Policy for Screw Jam 3D

Effective date: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Screw Jam 3D: Nuts & Bolts (the "Game") uses. The Game is provided by the developer of Screw Jam 3D ("we", "us"). You can contact us at simso688@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts.

The Game saves your progress (levels completed, stars, streaks, daily challenge history) and your sound setting on your own device (browser local storage or app storage). This information stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Where the Game is hosted

If you play the web version, the files are delivered by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages). Like any web host, it may log technical data such as your IP address and browser type in its server logs, under its own privacy policy. We do not receive or use this data to identify you.

Advertising

Versions of the Game that include ads show optional "rewarded" ads (for example, to unlock an extra slot). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interactions to deliver, measure and personalize ads, as described in Google's policies (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before ads are personalized.

Versions of the Game without ads (including any demo version) do not collect advertising data.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Your choices and rights

You can delete the data the Game stores on your device by clearing the site data (web) or by uninstalling the app. We do not sell your personal information. Depending on where you live (for example California, other U.S. states, the EEA or the UK), you may have rights to access, delete or opt out of certain uses of your information. Contact us at simso688@gmail.com and we will respond as required by law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
