## Full description
Looking for a satisfying brain teaser? Welcome to Screw Jam 3D: Nuts & Bolts, where logic meets relaxing, clicky mechanics!

Tap to unscrew colorful bolts, drop the tangled wooden and metal plates, and sort every bolt into the matching color box. Can you clear the whole board before your spare slots fill up?

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a bolt to unscrew it. Bolts covered by a plate can't be reached until the plate above is gone.
• Bolts that match the box on top fly straight in. Every other color waits in a spare slot.
• Fill a box with 3 bolts to send it off and open the next one.
• Don't let the spare slots fill up, or the board jams!
• Stuck? Use a Hint, smash any bolt with the Hammer, or watch an optional ad to unlock an extra slot.

FEATURES
• Satisfying 3D physics: plates swing on their last bolt and fall with real gravity.
• Endless levels: every level is checked to be solvable, from relaxed warm-ups to real brain-burners.
• Smooth, clicky sounds and gentle haptics.
• Works offline. No account, no sign-up.
• Color-blind friendly: every color has its own shape.
• Clean pastel look that is easy on the eyes.

Take a break, sharpen your mind and enjoy the clack. Download Screw Jam 3D: Nuts & Bolts and start unscrewing!
